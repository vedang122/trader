## Business (≤8 lines)
India's #2 telco (behind Jio by subs, ahead on ARPU), 681M subscribers across 15
countries (India + Africa). Revenue from mobile (voice/data), homes broadband,
enterprise/digital, DTH; ~50%-owned Indus Towers (equity method) is the tower
infra arm. Moat: spectrum licenses + national network + oligopoly market
structure (Jio, Airtel, distressed Vi) give real pricing power -- ARPU has
risen every quarter since the 2021-22 tariff-hike cycle began. Key structural
risk: capex-heavy, spectrum/AGR liabilities are the sector's classic
balance-sheet trap (see Red flags). Promoters (Bharti Telecom/Mittal family +
Singtel) hold ~50.07%, target is to consolidate toward >51% via buybacks/div.
