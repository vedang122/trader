## Business
India's #2 IT services/consulting major (behind TCS). Revenue from app
development/maintenance, BPM, consulting, cloud/data/AI transformation for
global enterprises (BFSI, retail, manufacturing, comms). Moat: US-India
labor-arbitrage delivery scale, long-tenure client relationships, domain
depth — this is the exact moat currently under structural test from
GenAI/agentic automation of billable-hour work. No dominant promoter:
founders (Murthy, Nilekani, Shibulal, Gopalakrishnan) hold ~13.8-14.5%
combined, diffuse, unpledged; professionally managed since 2014 (non-founder
CEOs). LIC holds ~11%, heavy FII/DII base.
