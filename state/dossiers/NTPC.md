## Business (≤8 lines)
India's largest power generator (Group installed capacity 85,637 MW, Q3
FY26), predominantly domestic-coal-fired thermal, majority of capacity under
long-term PPAs on CERC cost-plus tariff (assured ~15.5% RoE on each plant's
regulated equity base at PAF>=85%). Promoter Govt of India ~51.1%, no
pledging (sovereign promoter — not the same risk class as private-promoter
pledging). Moat = regulatory/capital-intensity entry barrier + captive PPA
book, not a pricing-power moat. Growth engine is NTPC Green Energy (listed
subsidiary) — competitively-bid renewables, NOT the same assured-RoE
structure as the thermal fleet, so its ramp changes the risk mix over time.
Fuel cost (incl. mandated imported-coal blending) is a genuine CERC
Regulation-43 pass-through with quarterly true-up — crude/coal spikes are
near-irrelevant to core P&L, unlike a merchant generator.
