## Business
India's #1 SUV maker by revenue market share (25.3%, FY26; volume share 14.1%,
up from 6.54% FY20) via Scorpio/XUV/Thar/Bolero, plus emerging EVs (BE6,
XEV9e). Also #1 tractor maker by volume — record 43.6% domestic share in
Q4FY26 (43.61% full-year), farm EBIT margin 20.8% (+110bps). Two genuine
scale moats (rural dealer/service network, brand trust, product-cycle
execution), not a single-product bet. Sits atop a sprawling conglomerate
(Tech Mahindra, Mahindra Finance NBFC, Mahindra Lifespace, Mahindra Holidays,
Mahindra CIE, SML Mahindra, aerospace/defence) — most separately listed with
independent public shareholders, which tempers (not eliminates) the
playbook's "too many subsidiaries" caution. Promoter holding structurally
low (18.44%, professionally managed like Tata Group; FII 36%/MF 17%/
Insurance 10.7%) — no pledging found. Post-2020 capital-discipline
turnaround under Group CEO Anish Shah: exited SsangYong (2020-22) and ~15
underperforming group businesses — a real pruning track record, not just
talk.
