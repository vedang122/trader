## Business
India's largest bank by assets, ~22% deposit share / ~20% advances share. Government
of India is the "promoter" at ~55-57% (no pledging risk — govt doesn't pledge, but
structural political-patronage/policy-lending risk instead; see red flags). Universal
bank + listed subsidiaries (SBI Life, SBI Cards, SBI MF, SBI Cap) add sum-of-parts
value. Moat: unmatched branch/distribution network, low-cost deposit franchise (CASA
~39%), systemic "too big to fail" status (D-SIB). Judge via GNPA/NNPA trend, PCR,
credit growth vs deposit growth, CAR/CET1, ROA/ROE — not a manufacturing-style D/E
or CFO check.
