## Business
India's largest bank by assets/deposits (~22% deposit share, ~20% advances share).
Promoter = Government of India, 55.5% holding — no pledging possible/applicable
structurally (PSU), but carries PSU-specific tail risk: priority-sector lending
mandates, political-cycle influence on credit allocation, occasional "national
service" duty (past distressed-bank rescues). Universal-bank moat: unmatched
branch/distribution network, low-cost liability franchise, cross-sell via listed
subsidiaries (SBI Life, SBI Cards, SBI Funds Mgmt, SBI Capital Mkts — separately
governed, not related-party siphoning vehicles). Bank economics inherently
leveraged (regulated); judge via GNPA/NNPA/NIM/CAR, not non-financial D/E or CFO.
