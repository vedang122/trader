## Business
India's largest bank by assets/deposits/branches; ~57% GoI-owned — no promoter-pledging
risk is even possible (sovereign owner, can't secretly pledge). Moat: largest CASA/
deposit franchise (~23% system deposit share), pan-India branch network, quasi-sovereign
trust/implicit backstop. Subsidiaries (SBI Life, SBI Cards, SBI Funds Mgmt, SBI Capital)
are separately listed/governed — sum-of-parts value, not related-party siphoning
vehicles. Structural discount vs private peers is real: lower NIM (large low-yield
priority-sector book), thinner capital buffer, periodic political-directive-lending/
PSU-governance overhang. Judge earnings quality via GNPA/NNPA + CAR trend, not a
non-financial CFO/D-E check (leverage is the business model for a bank).
