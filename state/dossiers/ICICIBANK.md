## Business
India's #2 private-sector bank. No single promoter — institutionally held (FII 33.8%,
DII 42.3%, Public 23.7%, Govt 0.2%) since the 2002 ICICI Ltd restructuring; this removes
promoter-pledging risk structurally. Moat: scale, brand trust, digital/retail franchise,
pan-India distribution. Subsidiaries add sum-of-parts value — ICICI Prudential Life,
ICICI Lombard General Insurance, ICICI Securities, ICICI AMC — all separately listed/
governed, not related-party siphoning vehicles. Bank economics are inherently leveraged
(regulated); judge earnings quality via GNPA/NNPA trend + capital adequacy, not a
non-financial D/E or CFO check.
