## Business (≤8 lines)
Diversified conglomerate: Cigarettes (cash engine, ~55%+ segment EBIT margin,
licensed oligopoly moat with VST/Godfrey Phillips as only other organized
players), FMCG-Others (branded packaged foods/personal care, growing but
thinner margin), Agri-business, Paperboards & Packaging. Hotels demerged into
separate listed entity ITC Hotels (~Jan 2025) — post-demerger quarters are the
correct YoY base. No promoter group; BAT holds ~23% as largest institutional
shareholder, no pledging. Debt-free / net cash (~₹213bn net cash, D/E ~0).
Moat: govt-licensed cigarette oligopoly + India's largest FMCG distribution
network (rural+urban reach few peers match).
