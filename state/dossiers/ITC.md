## Business (≤8 lines)
Diversified conglomerate: cigarettes (~50%+ of segment PBIT, dominant legal-market
share, oligopoly with Godfrey Phillips a distant #2 — regulatory/licensing barrier
is the real moat), FMCG-other (foods, personal care - growing, thin ~7% PBT
margin), paperboard/packaging, agribusiness. Hotels demerged Jan-2025 (no longer
consolidated). No promoter group under Indian classification; largest single
shareholder is BAT plc (~25%, periodically trims via block deals to fund its own
deleveraging — e.g. sold 9% of ITC Hotels for ~Rs3,820cr in late 2025). DII 49%,
FII 34%, public 17% (screener.in, Jun-2026). Virtually debt-free, high dividend
payout (74.5%, ~5% yield) — professionally managed, no known governance/pledging
issues.
