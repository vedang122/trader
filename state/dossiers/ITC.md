## Business
Cigarettes (ITC/Wills/Gold Flake) = the cash engine: legal oligopoly (ITC,
Godfrey Phillips, VST), high pricing power but earnings are hostage to
GST/excise policy — this is the single biggest swing factor in ITC's P&L.
FMCG-Others = large revenue, thin margin (~7% segment margin), still not a
standalone profit engine. Hotels demerged into ITC Hotels Ltd, effective
1-Jan-2025, listed 29-Jan-2025; ITC retains a 40% equity-accounted stake
(not consolidated) — Q1FY26 was already the first post-demerger base, so
Q1FY27 YoY comps are like-for-like on this front. Agri + Paperboards round
out a diversified but cigarette-dependent profit mix. No controlling
promoter — BAT is the largest single shareholder (~25.5%); pledging is
moot. Fortress balance sheet: net cash, strong FCF, ~5% dividend yield.
