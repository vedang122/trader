## Business (≤8 lines — stable facts only)
Post ITC Hotels demerger (Jan 2025), ITC = Cigarettes + FMCG-Others (foods,
personal care) + Paperboards/Packaging + Agri. Cigarettes still drive the
bulk of segment profit despite being a minority of revenue; dominant legal
cigarette market share, iconic brands (Wills, Gold Flake). Moat: oligopoly
in legal cigarettes + massive FMCG distribution reach + near-zero debt.
No controlling Indian promoter; BAT (via TMI/Myddleton/Rothmans) holds
~22-23% and has been steadily trimming via disclosed block trades (not
clandestine). Professionally managed, board-run (Chairman Sanjiv Puri).

## Red flags & watch items (≤6 lines)
- Feb-2026 tobacco tax overhaul (40% GST + excise restructure) — live,
  ongoing margin compression in cigarette segment through FY27.
- NCCD statutory rate raised 25%→60% (effective duty held at 25% via

- BAT continuing to trim stake via block trades — orderly/disclosed, not a
  governance red flag, but a persistent supply overhang on the float.
- Watch for volume erosion / illicit-trade share shift as price hikes
  (~17%) get tested against demand elasticity over next 2-3 quarters.
