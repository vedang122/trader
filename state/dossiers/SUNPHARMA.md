## Business (≤8 lines)
India's largest pharma company by revenue. Three legs: domestic branded
generics (India #1, high-margin), global generics (US/EM/ROW), and a growing
specialty portfolio (Ilumya, Winlevi, Cequa) which carries the best margins
and is the market's growth narrative. Moat: branded-generic distribution
scale in India + specialty pipeline optionality, not a hard patent moat.
Promoter Dilip Shanghvi family holds 54.5%, stable, no pledging found.
Key risk: Halol plant has a multi-year history of USFDA compliance lapses
(warning letters 2015/2016, OAI classification + fresh warning letter
Sept 2025) — a recurring quality-system issue at one facility, not the whole
company.
