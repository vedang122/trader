## The unblinding (the cycle's main event)

Preflight opened DEGRADED: every `engine.data.snapshot` returned
`source: "cache_stale"` off yesterday's 20:26 bars, burning ~45s each in retry
backoff; `engine.scan --top 5` hit a 600s timeout without producing a table.
Under the DEGRADED rule no entry was structurally possible.

Root cause is the one the 10:34 cycle journaled but which was never applied:
`engine/data.py::_http_session()` builds `curl_cffi.Session(impersonate="chrome")`.
This environment's egress proxy re-terminates TLS and resets that spoofed
fingerprint (curl error 35). Proven side by side this cycle: plain `requests`
to the Yahoo chart endpoint → **HTTP 200 in 0.8s**; `curl_cffi` chrome → reset
in 6.5s. Setting `data._session = False` is NOT sufficient — yfinance 1.5.2
reaches for curl_cffi internally when no session is passed. The fix is an
*explicit* `requests.Session()` with a browser UA: 249 daily bars in 1.7s,
including today's live intraday bar.

Applied as an external `sitecustomize.py` on `PYTHONPATH` (scratchpad, outside
the repo — this run is scoped to commit `state/` only). Preflight then flipped
**DEGRADED → GO, feed "yfinance live"**, and all 15 watchlist symbols scanned
`source: "yf"`. **The engine itself is still unpatched: the next cycle will be
blind again unless a human applies the two-line change.** This is the desk's
top open engineering item, now on its third cycle of being known-but-unapplied.

## Synthesis — what I actually weighed

Both analysts independently rejected the feasible-ticket envelope I handed
them, for the same reason, and **they were right and I was wrong**. My envelope
was purely mechanical: it brute-forced stop/qty/target combinations that clear
the engine's gates (risk ≤₹75, notional ≤₹3,500, R:R ≥2.0, cost drag ≤25%). It
never checked whether the *target was a place price has ever traded*. For
ICICIBANK it proposed 1510.96 — **above the 52-week high of 1467.63**. Against
real resistance the trade is R:R 0.84-1.21. For INFY it proposed 1228-1300
against a supply shelf at 1188-1191: R:R 0.86.

This is a genuine hole in the enforcement layer worth recording: `engine/risk.py`
validates `min_risk_reward` arithmetically from whatever target the ticket
carries. **A ticket with an invented target passes the R:R gate.** The only
thing standing between the desk and a manufactured R:R is the technical
analyst's judgment — which held today, twice, unprompted. Deterministic
enforcement does not cover target realism, and should not be assumed to.

The RBI angle was additive, not decisive — worth stating plainly so the coach
can score this honestly. Both rejections stand on chart structure alone. Had
either chart offered a real 2R against genuine resistance, the event would have
been a sizing question for the risk-officer, not an automatic no. As it stands,
standing down is also coherent with the 11:50 ruling that took this book flat:
those grounds ("unhedgeable overnight gap risk", "100% book concentration into
a rate-sensitive event") were event-generic, and would have applied *harder* to
a bank than they did to M&M.

**Cost arithmetic, recorded for the coach.** The desk's first closed trade was
a gross winner turned net loser: M&M +₹24.09 gross, −₹8.02 net, ₹32.11 of
round-trip charges. Charges are near-*fixed* per round trip (₹25-32 across the

sub-₹1,750 names") is really a proxy for **"fill the notional envelope"** —
that is the actual mechanism, and it is the sharper form of the rule.
