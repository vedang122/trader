## New data-integrity finding (third class, distinct from the known two)
`snapshot()` exposes `last` and `change_pct` (vs prior close) but NOT today's
open/high/low. A gap-and-fade — the single most informative shape in an
earnings reaction — is therefore INVISIBLE to any agent reading snapshot alone.
The desk saw "+0.18%, boring" when the truth was "+2.5% gap, fully retraced".
Both news-analyst and macro-sentinel formed their reads on that sanitised
number; macro-sentinel specifically cited BHARTIARTL "flipping green" as
evidence of caution unwinding, when it was in fact being distributed.
Recommended engine fix: add day_open/day_high/day_low (or gap_pct and
pct_off_day_high) to the snapshot payload.
