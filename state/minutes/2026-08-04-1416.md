## Synthesis — what I weighed

Both names failed the same gate, and the shape of the failure is the point.
At ₹10,000 of capital the 25% cost-viability rule sets a *floor on how far the
target must be*: roughly ₹100 of gross gain per position, which on a ₹1,000–2,000
share is a 5%+ move. That floor does not care where resistance actually sits.
The temptation this creates is precise and dangerous — pick the target that
clears the gate, then hunt for a chart story to justify it. The SBIN analyst
named that trap explicitly and refused it; SUNPHARMA's cost-viable target was
literally a new all-time-range high three sessions after that exact level was
rejected on heavy volume. **Neither trade was killed by the RBI event. Both
were killed by the chart, before event risk was even added** — which is the
honest version, and a better reason to stand down than event-caution would
have been.
